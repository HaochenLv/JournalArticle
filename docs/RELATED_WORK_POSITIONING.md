# External positioning checks

Checked 2026-09-17. These are scope checks, not an exhaustive novelty survey.

- The [official HELIX artifact](https://github.com/Thesys-lab/Helix-ASPLOS25) explicitly includes both simulator and prototype paths. Our imports select the simulator; no physical-system validation should be inferred.
- [Vidur's official implementation](https://github.com/microsoft/vidur) already supports LLM inference capacity planning and configuration search. “CPU-only simulator-guided deployment planning” alone is not new. The [MLSys paper](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/b74a8de47d2b3c928360e0a011f48351-Abstract-Conference.html) is the primary comparison for scope and validation practice. Published and preprint abstracts contain different aggregate error figures; do not mix them into a numerical comparison with our one-model diagnostics.
- [Robust ranking and selection with optimal computing budget allocation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2017.03.019) already studies finite scenario uncertainty and fixed simulation budgets. A generic reference-call budget or finite-scenario worst case is not a new optimization principle. Our potential contribution is a small, tested planning procedure for TTFT/TPOT-constrained layer pipelines with explicit nominal model disagreement, sparse validation, and finite-trace safe islands. No probability-of-correct-selection theory is claimed.
- [Multi-Fidelity Simulation Modeling for Discrete Event Simulation](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9775096/) establishes that speed/accuracy trade-offs in multiple simulation fidelities are prior work. A journal introduction must position this work as a specific decision-validation study rather than inventing multi-fidelity optimization.

The Future Internet author-instructions endpoint returned HTTP 429 during this audit. Do not invent a journal-specific percentage-of-new-material threshold. Before submission, verify the current official [author instructions](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/futureinternet/instructions), explicitly cite both conference papers, and disclose their relationship and the new material in the submission. No acceptance guarantee is inferred from journal fit.
